Remo is a Hinglish and Marathi voice memory. Tell it your day, your work, your farm, your shop — and days later it answers where you left off and what changed while you were away, out loud, in your language.

Try it in your browser

Runs anywhere: macOS, Windows, Linux — and any phone, Android and iOS included.

How a day with Remo sounds

  1. Evening, two minutes. “Yaad rakhna, issue 214: login fix ho gaya, docs baaki hai.” Stored — no questions asked.
  2. Days later. “Issue 214 mein kya bacha hai?” Remo answers with what is pending first.
  3. Welcome back. “Koi update aaya?” Remo briefs what changed on the watched issue since you were last here — with links.

What it does

  • Keyword saving. “Yaad rakhna”, “remember”, or “lakshat thev” stores instantly. Undo is one sentence away.
  • Work memory. Done vs pending, per project and issue — for students, farmers, shopkeepers, and health workers alike.
  • Change watcher. Point Remo at a GitHub issue or repo; it reports only what is new.
  • Reminders that speak. Hinglish times (“saade nau”, “5 minute baad”), chimed and spoken, surviving restarts.
  • Quiz + day plan. Tested on your own notes; briefed every morning.
  • Learns your voice. Your vocabulary boosts recognition; stubborn mishearings become fixes.

Your memory stays yours

Notes, reminders, and keys live on your own machine. Voice goes to Gnani for transcription, text goes to Gnani for speech, and nothing else leaves. The demo uses made-up data only.

Start in two minutes

  1. Copy .env.example to .env and paste a key from app.gnani.ai/voice.
  2. Run pip install -r requirements.txt, then uvicorn server:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000.
  3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000, press the mic, and say “yaad rakhna…”.
